The problem

Half of B2B invoices are overdue — and the reasons are buried in email.

Disputes, short payments and promised dates live in inboxes and spreadsheets. By the time someone pieces them together, the follow-up is late and the tone is wrong.

Financial safeguards

AI assistance with accounting discipline

InvoiceHarbor is designed so that a model can never be the reason a balance is wrong or a message is sent.

Recorded data is authoritativeBalances are invoice totals minus verified payments. Models never produce or adjust figures.
No paid status without proofAn invoice moves to paid only when bank-verified payments cover it. Remittance advice stays “unconfirmed”.
No payment initiationThe platform has no capability to move money, charge cards or initiate transfers.
Approval before any messageDrafts need a named approver; sensitive collection messages need explicit acknowledgement. Staff send from their own mailbox.
Amount validationAny dollar figure in a draft that doesn’t match a recorded figure blocks approval automatically.
Full audit trailEvery classification, draft, edit, approval and verification is logged with who and when.

FAQ

Questions finance teams ask us

Can InvoiceHarbor send reminders automatically?

No. Every reminder is a draft until a named team member approves it, and sensitive collection messages (dispute-related, firm or escalated) need an extra acknowledgement. Even after approval, your staff send the message from their own mailbox and record it as sent. Automatic sending is locked off.

Will the AI ever change a balance or mark an invoice paid?

No. Balances are always computed as recorded invoice totals minus verified payments. An invoice becomes paid only when bank-verified payments cover it. Remittance advice or a customer’s “we paid” email is recorded as an unconfirmed claim and does not reduce the balance.

How is the AI kept from inventing numbers in reminders?

Each draft is generated from a list of permitted figures tied to their source records. Before approval, every dollar amount in the message is checked against that list; anything that doesn’t match blocks approval.

What’s the difference between estimated and verified payment timing?

Verified payment information comes from confirmed bank records. Estimated timing is a projection based on a customer’s verified history, always labelled as an estimate and shown separately. Disputed invoices get no estimate at all.

Which invoice formats can you extract?

Supported PDF and image invoices processed by Amazon Textract AnalyzeExpense. Extracted fields carry confidence scores and are checked arithmetically; low-confidence or inconsistent results are routed to human review instead of becoming records automatically.

Do you use our data to train AI models?

No. Amazon Bedrock does not use customer prompts or outputs to train foundation models, and InvoiceHarbor does not either. Documents and records stay encrypted in your tenant.

How does the HubSpot integration work?

HubSpot manages prospects, demo bookings, onboarding and the sales pipeline. Financial records stay in InvoiceHarbor. Any synchronization is limited to non-financial fields and only when an authorized integration explicitly requires it.

Does InvoiceHarbor initiate payments or store card details?

No. InvoiceHarbor coordinates receivables; it has no capability to initiate payments, move money, or store payment credentials.
